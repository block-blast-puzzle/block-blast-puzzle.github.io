Game Objective

The goal in Block Blast Puzzle is to score as many points as possible by fitting blocks efficiently, clearing multiple lines at once, and avoiding gridlock. Smart planning and strategy are key to achieving high scores.

Tips for High Scores

  • Leave Space: Keep room for larger block shapes.
  • Clear Multiple Lines: Clearing more than one line at once earns extra points.
